# Privacy Policy

**U Converter** converts files and reads text from PDF, PNG, and JPG on your Mac. Effective 1 September 2026.

## On this Mac only

The app does not create an account. Convert does not upload your files.

## What we read

When you add a file (Open, Open Folder, or drag and drop), the app reads it to extract text.

## What we store

Converted `.txt` files are saved in the History folder you choose. The default is `~/Library/Application Support/Image to Text/History`. Original images are not copied into History.

## Network

OCR runs on a local server at `127.0.0.1`. Other computers cannot use it. The first time the model is missing, it may download from Hugging Face, then stay on disk for offline use. Hugging Face telemetry is disabled. Convert itself does not send your pages to the internet.

## What we don’t collect

No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or usage profiles.

## Your control

Delete a file or batch from History, or use **Settings → Delete All History**. Uninstalling the app does not remove the History folder unless you delete it.
